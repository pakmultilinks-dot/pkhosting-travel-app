Frequently asked questions

How do I book a flight on Travel.pk?

Search your route and dates, compare the returned itineraries and fare conditions, choose an offer, then contact the Travel.pk team to book it. Call +92 42 3569 2952, email info@travel.pk, or message on WhatsApp, Monday to Friday 10 AM to 7 PM PKT, in Urdu or English. Have every passenger name ready. The team confirms the live fare and the fare conditions before you pay anything.

What payment methods does Travel.pk accept?

The Travel.pk team confirms the payment methods for your booking before you pay. Some bookings may be paid by card, wallet or manually verified local transfer. Do not send money to details that are not given to you by the Travel.pk team.

Can I cancel or change my flight?

It depends on the airline and fare conditions. Contact the Travel.pk team to ask for a change or cancellation. Read the quoted airline penalty and any fare difference before confirming a change or cancellation.

Does Travel.pk charge booking fees?

The team confirms the full amount due before you pay. Ask them to break down the fare, optional extras, service amount and any payment cost. If the total differs from what you expected, stop and ask support before paying.

What if my booking remains in processing?

Do not immediately submit the same booking again. Check your email first, then contact support with the booking reference so the team can confirm whether the airline issued the order.
